About Us

A School Rooted in Values

Shiv Sakti Vidhyalaya, Odekera is a CGBSE Board affiliated school dedicated to the local community, focused on holistic development — academics, discipline, sports, and character building go hand in hand here. Our teachers work closely with every child, ensuring no student is left behind.

We believe in celebrating small wins — every improved grade, every act of kindness, and every milestone — because that is how confident, future-ready children are built.

"Our aim is simple: every child who walks through our gates should leave more confident, more capable, and more curious than when they arrived." — Principal's Message

Join Our School

A simple, transparent admission process for parents.

Enquiry

Visit the school office or call us to enquire about seat availability for your child's class.

Form Submission

Confirmation

Once confirmed, complete the fee formalities to secure your child's seat for the academic year.

Frequently Asked Questions

What classes does the school offer?+
We currently offer classes from Nursery through Class 8 (CGBSE State Board).
What documents are needed for admission?+
Birth certificate, previous school records (if applicable), and passport-size photographs of the child.
Is transport facility available?+
Yes, transport is available for students residing in nearby areas. Please contact the school office for route details.
What are the school office hours?+
The school office is open from 9 AM to 3 PM, Monday through Saturday.
How can I pay the school fees?+
Fees can be paid directly at the school office. Please refer to the downloadable Fee Structure document for details.
